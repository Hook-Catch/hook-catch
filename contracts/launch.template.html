$HOOKCATCH setup

Run these three steps from the wallet that deployed Hook Catch, after $HOOKCATCH launches on Pons.

1. Holder perks —

setCatchToken: holders of 100K get a 0.10% fee and unlimited rules, 1M pay 0%. Can be set only once.

2. Buyback & burn contract —

Deploys HookCatchBuyback for $HOOKCATCH: the fees it receives can only buy $HOOKCATCH and burn it. No owner, no withdraw. Buys 0.003–0.05 ETH at a time, on the curve and after graduation in the v4 pool.

3. Send fees to the buyback —

setFeeSink: from now on every protocol fee goes to the buyback contract. The keeper bot runs the buyback when 0.003 ETH has piled up.
